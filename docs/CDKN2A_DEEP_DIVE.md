# CDKN2A Deep Dive

CDKN2A is retained as a priority candidate even though no individual CpG passes FDR in the current Ref2 cohort.

The expanded workflow does not reduce CDKN2A to one CpG. It evaluates:
- all retained CDKN2A CpGs;
- beta-value distributions;
- delta-beta magnitude;
- promoter/body context;
- direction consistency;
- leave-one-non-progressor-out stability;
- within-patient lesion trajectories;
- probe retention after QC.

This is a regional/effect-size interpretation, not a claim of a validated methylation biomarker.
