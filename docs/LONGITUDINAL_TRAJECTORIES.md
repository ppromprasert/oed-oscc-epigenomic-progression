# Longitudinal Lesion Trajectories

Longitudinal analysis was rebuilt around specimen-level pathology and chronology.

The audit found no clean methylation-profiled dysplasia specimen followed later by a methylation-profiled SCC specimen.

Three patients had evaluable Mild-to-Severe dysplasia trajectories.

To avoid pseudoreplication, multiple Severe specimens collected on the same date are averaged within patient and grade.

Across the three trajectories:

- malathion-associated methylation score decreased in all three;
- atrazine-associated score decreased in all three;
- the clinical progressor showed a metolachlor increase of +2.41 standardized-score units;
- the clinical progressor also showed an arsenic increase of +1.28.

With n = 3, these are descriptive case-level patterns only.
