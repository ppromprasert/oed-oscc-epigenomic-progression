# Exposure-Associated Methylation Signatures

Nine usable signatures were scored:

- arsenic
- atrazine
- cadmium
- heptachlor
- malathion
- metolachlor
- PAH / combustion
- PM2.5
- smoking

Scores are direction-oriented averages of standardized methylation values at literature-linked CpGs.

They are **exposure-associated epigenetic patterns**, not direct measurements of environmental dose.

Signature support varies substantially by retained CpG coverage. Multi-CpG signatures with stronger coverage carry more interpretive weight than single-CpG exploratory markers.

In the current Ref2 comparison, no exposure signature reached nominal P < 0.05 or BH FDR < 0.05.

The smallest P values were PAH/combustion and smoking, both P = 0.0876 and FDR = 0.356.

Across all 92 samples, cadmium-associated score versus CXCL10 promoter methylation was the strongest current exposure/candidate correlation (rho = 0.340; P = 0.000906; FDR = 0.0245).

Correlated signatures can share CpGs or reflect overlapping biological responses, so they should not be interpreted as independent causal exposure effects.
