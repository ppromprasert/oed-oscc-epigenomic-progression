# Gene- and Genomic-Region Summaries

CpGs are assigned to:
- TSS200
- TSS1500
- first exon
- 5'UTR
- gene body
- 3'UTR
- other

Gene-region summaries report:
- number of probes;
- nominal and FDR-significant probes;
- median delta beta;
- median percent methylation difference;
- percentage of positive/negative limma effects;
- direction-consistency percentage;
- minimum P and FDR.

This is important because promoter methylation, gene-body methylation, and isolated CpG effects have different biological interpretations.
