# Real Project Aggregate Results

These are aggregate, non-patient-level results from the underlying research analysis.

- Known OSCC set: no CpG reached FDR < 0.05. The strongest nominal CpG was **CDH1 cg01251360** (limma logFC -3.61, P=7.37e-4, FDR=0.252).
- Other biologically plausible exploratory genes included CDKN2A, EGFR, DAPK1, ATG5, TFPI2 and MGMT.
- Expanded CDKN2A effect-size review found a broad tendency toward lower methylation in progressors; this should be interpreted as effect-size patterning rather than FDR-significant evidence.
- CosMx set: **UBD cg13206902** was the only CpG reaching FDR < 0.05 in the exploratory Ref2 analysis (logFC 1.96, P=1.32e-4, FDR=0.047), with higher methylation in progressors.
- CosMx promoter analyses showed substantial sample-specific heterogeneity in immune/interferon genes.
- The dominant limitation is the very small non-progressor group; results are hypothesis-generating.
